I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science and Technology at [Fudan University](https://www.fudan.edu.cn/en/), advised by Prof. Wenchao Ding. Before joining Fudan, I received my B.Eng. in Software Engineering from the University of Electronic Science and Technology of China, where I worked with Prof. Yong Liao.

My research focuses on **embodied AI and robot manipulation**. I am interested in visuomotor policy learning, imitation and reinforcement learning, generative and multimodal models, action representations, world models, and long-horizon decision making.

I work across simulation and real-robot systems, including Franka and AgileX single- and dual-arm platforms. My recent work studies stage-adaptive motion speed and memory-based temporal reasoning for robot manipulation.
