I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science and Technology at [Fudan University](https://www.fudan.edu.cn/en/), advised by [Prof. Wenchao Ding](https://wenchaoding.github.io/). Before joining Fudan, I received my B.Eng. in Software Engineering from the University of Electronic Science and Technology of China, where I was advised by [Prof. Yong Liao](https://sise.uestc.edu.cn/info/1035/5650.htm).

My research focuses on **embodied AI and robot manipulation**. I work across simulation and real-robot systems. My recent work studies stage-adaptive motion speed **learning** and memory-based temporal reasoning for robot manipulation.
